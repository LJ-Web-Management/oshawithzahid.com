Why learn OSHA safety in Spanish?

Safety training only works if it's understood. Workers who learn in their first language catch details that get lost in translation: what a guardrail height really means, why a ladder angle matters, what a chemical label is warning about. They also ask more questions, which is where real learning happens.

OSHA itself requires that training be presented in a language and vocabulary workers understand. For crews where Spanish is the first language, a Spanish-language class is one of the clearest ways to meet that expectation.

Frequently asked questions

Is an OSHA card from a Spanish-language class official?

Yes. OSHA allows authorized trainers to teach Outreach courses in languages other than English. Graduates receive the same U.S. Department of Labor wallet card as an English-language class.

Can my crew mix Spanish and English speakers in one class?

Yes. Zahid can teach in English and explain key points in Spanish so everyone in a mixed group follows along.

Do you teach Spanish OSHA classes in other states?

Yes. On-site classes are available nationwide with as little as a week's notice. Share your location and group size through the booking form.

Are the course materials in Spanish?

Instruction, discussion, and explanations are in Spanish. Where a handout or OSHA standard is only available in English, Zahid explains it in Spanish as the class goes.
