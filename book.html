Booking FAQ

Common questions about scheduling

How quickly can I schedule a session?

Most sessions can be booked within a week. Fill out the booking form with your group size and preferred dates, and Zahid will follow up to confirm availability.

Do you offer group rates for OSHA training?

Yes. Group pricing is available for crews of any size. Include your headcount in the request form and you will receive a custom quote.

Can training be done on-site at our location?

Yes. On-site training is available at your job site, shop, or office, in addition to scheduled group sessions.

What do I need to prepare before the session?

Just a space for the group and a list of attendee names for the certification cards. Zahid handles course materials and testing.
