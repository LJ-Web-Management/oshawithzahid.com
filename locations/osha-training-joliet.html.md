## What Joliet employers should know

Warehouses and logistics operations are General Industry workplaces, where forklift (powered industrial truck) safety, walking-working surfaces, and hazard communication are frequent citation areas. The large construction projects around Joliet are Construction workplaces, where falls, struck-by, and caught-in hazards dominate. Many companies here need both: OSHA 10 General Industry for operations and OSHA 10 or 30 Construction for project crews.

**Plan ahead:** a legitimate OSHA 10 takes at least two days and OSHA 30 at least four, because OSHA limits Outreach training to 7.5 hours per day. Any "one-day OSHA 10" offer is a red flag. See [how to spot a fake card](https://oshawithzahid.com/guides/how-to-verify-osha-card.html).
