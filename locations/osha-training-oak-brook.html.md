## What Oak Brook employers should know

Building engineers and maintenance techs face real hazards: electrical work, ladders and roofs, chemicals, confined spaces, and hazardous energy on mechanical equipment. OSHA 10 or 30 General Industry gives them a framework for working safely, and OSHA 30 prepares chief engineers and property managers to oversee contractors and compliance.

**Plan ahead:** a legitimate OSHA 10 takes at least two days and OSHA 30 at least four, because OSHA limits Outreach training to 7.5 hours per day. Any "one-day OSHA 10" offer is a red flag. See [how to spot a fake card](https://oshawithzahid.com/guides/how-to-verify-osha-card.html).
