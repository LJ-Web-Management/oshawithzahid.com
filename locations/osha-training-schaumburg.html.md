## What Schaumburg employers should know

Hotels, retail, and office buildings are General Industry workplaces. Their biggest OSHA risks are often less obvious than a construction site's: ladders and slips, electrical work by maintenance staff, chemicals used in housekeeping and pools, and lockout/tagout on building equipment. Contractors working inside those buildings fall under Construction rules, so many Schaumburg properties need both courses.

**Plan ahead:** a legitimate OSHA 10 takes at least two days and OSHA 30 at least four, because OSHA limits Outreach training to 7.5 hours per day. Any "one-day OSHA 10" offer is a red flag. See [how to spot a fake card](https://oshawithzahid.com/guides/how-to-verify-osha-card.html).

### Do hotel maintenance workers need OSHA 10?

It's not legally required for every worker, but OSHA 10 General Industry is an excellent baseline for hotel maintenance and engineering staff, and OSHA standards require training on specific hazards like chemicals and electrical work.

### Should a tenant build-out crew take Construction or General Industry?

Construction. Build-out, renovation, and remodeling work falls under 29 CFR 1926.
