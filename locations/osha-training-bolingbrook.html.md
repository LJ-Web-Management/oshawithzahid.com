## What Bolingbrook employers should know

Most Bolingbrook employers are General Industry workplaces, where forklift safety, dock edges, racking, and hazard communication are top concerns. OSHA 10 General Industry gives warehouse workers a solid baseline, and OSHA 30 General Industry prepares shift leads and supervisors to manage safety across a building.

**Plan ahead:** a legitimate OSHA 10 takes at least two days and OSHA 30 at least four, because OSHA limits Outreach training to 7.5 hours per day. Any "one-day OSHA 10" offer is a red flag. See [how to spot a fake card](https://oshawithzahid.com/guides/how-to-verify-osha-card.html).
