## What Naperville employers should know

Naperville straddles DuPage and Will counties. Private employers are covered by federal OSHA, and public employers such as the city, school districts, and park district fall under Illinois OSHA. Either way, the training content is the same, and OSHA 10 or 30 is a strong foundation for the hazard-specific training OSHA standards require.

**Plan ahead:** a legitimate OSHA 10 takes at least two days and OSHA 30 at least four, because OSHA limits Outreach training to 7.5 hours per day. Any "one-day OSHA 10" offer is a red flag. See [how to spot a fake card](https://oshawithzahid.com/guides/how-to-verify-osha-card.html).
