## What Chicago employers should know

Private employers in Chicago are covered by federal OSHA, while City of Chicago and other public employees are covered by Illinois OSHA. Illinois has no blanket state law requiring OSHA 10 cards, but in Chicago the requirement almost always arrives through the contract: owners, general contractors, unions, and public agencies write OSHA 10 and OSHA 30 into bid documents and site rules. If you are bidding on larger Chicago work, get your crew carded before mobilization.

**Plan ahead:** a legitimate OSHA 10 takes at least two days and OSHA 30 at least four, because OSHA limits Outreach training to 7.5 hours per day. Any "one-day OSHA 10" offer is a red flag. See [how to spot a fake card](https://oshawithzahid.com/guides/how-to-verify-osha-card.html).
