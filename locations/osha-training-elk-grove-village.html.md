## What Elk Grove Village employers should know

With so many manufacturers in one place, the industrial park sees the full range of General Industry hazards: machine guarding, lockout/tagout, forklifts, hazard communication, and electrical. Data center and industrial construction projects here frequently require OSHA 10 for workers and OSHA 30 for supervisors.

**Plan ahead:** a legitimate OSHA 10 takes at least two days and OSHA 30 at least four, because OSHA limits Outreach training to 7.5 hours per day. Any "one-day OSHA 10" offer is a red flag. See [how to spot a fake card](https://oshawithzahid.com/guides/how-to-verify-osha-card.html).
