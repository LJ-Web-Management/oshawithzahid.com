## What Aurora employers should know

Federal OSHA has an area office in the Aurora area that handles inspections for many western-suburb employers. Common citations in industrial settings include hazard communication, lockout/tagout, powered industrial trucks, and machine guarding, all covered in OSHA 10 and 30 General Industry. Construction crews in Aurora see the same fall protection and ladder citations as the rest of the country.

**Plan ahead:** a legitimate OSHA 10 takes at least two days and OSHA 30 at least four, because OSHA limits Outreach training to 7.5 hours per day. Any "one-day OSHA 10" offer is a red flag. See [how to spot a fake card](https://oshawithzahid.com/guides/how-to-verify-osha-card.html).
