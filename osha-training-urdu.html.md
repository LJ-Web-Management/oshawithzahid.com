## Why take OSHA training in Urdu?

Many workers can get by in English on the job, but safety training is where "getting by" isn't enough. Learning in Urdu means workers understand _why_ a rule exists, not just what it says, and they're far more likely to speak up when something looks wrong.

OSHA expects employers to present training in a language workers understand. An Urdu-language Outreach course meets that expectation and gives every worker the same official card.
