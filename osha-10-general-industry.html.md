## What is OSHA 10-Hour General Industry?

OSHA 10-Hour General Industry is the entry-level Outreach course for **every workplace that isn't a construction site**: distribution centers, manufacturing plants, machine shops, food processing, hospitals and clinics, hotels, retail stores, and building maintenance. It teaches workers to recognize the hazards most often behind injuries and OSHA citations in those settings.

## What the course covers

The required core topics are set by OSHA:

- **Introduction to OSHA**: worker rights, employer responsibilities, and how to report hazards
- **Walking and working surfaces**: slips, trips, falls, ladders, and dock edges
- **Exit routes, emergency action plans, and fire prevention**
- **Electrical safety**
- **Personal protective equipment**
- **Hazard communication**: labels, safety data sheets, and chemical safety

Elective time is matched to your operation. Common choices include **powered industrial trucks (forklifts), machine guarding, lockout/tagout, materials handling, ergonomics, and bloodborne pathogens**.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between OSHA 10 General Industry and OSHA 10 Construction?

General Industry follows 29 CFR 1910 and is for fixed workplaces like warehouses, plants, hospitals, and stores. Construction follows 29 CFR 1926 and is for building, renovation, and demolition work. Pick the one that matches where you work most.

### Can you schedule OSHA 10 around our shifts?

Yes. Classes can run across two full days or be split into shorter sessions that fit first, second, or third shift, as long as no day exceeds OSHA's 7.5-hour limit.

### Does OSHA 10 General Industry cover forklifts?

Powered industrial trucks can be included as an elective. Note that OSHA 10 does not replace the separate forklift operator training and evaluation OSHA requires under 1910.178.

### How many workers can attend one class?

OSHA limits Outreach classes to 40 students per trainer. For larger workforces, Zahid can run several sessions back to back.
