On-site OSHA training means an OSHA-authorized Outreach Trainer comes to your job site, shop, or office and teaches OSHA 10 or OSHA 30 to your group in person. Zahid Presswala offers on-site Construction and General Industry classes throughout Chicagoland, with group pricing, flexible scheduling, instruction in English, Gujarati, or Urdu, and an official DOL wallet card for every graduate.

## At a glance

- All four OSHA Outreach courses: 10 and 30-Hour, Construction and General Industry
- At your job site, shop, office, or a shared venue
- Up to 40 students per class (OSHA limit); multiple sessions for larger teams
- Group pricing, so the per-worker cost drops as headcount grows
- Taught in English, Gujarati, or Urdu

## How on-site OSHA training works

1. **Tell us what you need.** Use the [booking form](https://oshawithzahid.com/book.html) to share your industry, headcount, location, language, and dates.
2. **Get a quote and a plan.** Zahid confirms the right course, picks electives that match your hazards, and sets a schedule that fits your shifts.
3. **Train on-site.** Your team completes the course where they work, with examples drawn from your equipment and conditions.
4. **Get carded.** Each graduate gets proof of completion, and official DOL wallet cards follow (OSHA requires them within 90 days).

## Frequently asked questions

### What do we need to provide for an on-site class?

A room or trailer that seats your group, tables or chairs, and a wall or screen for slides. Zahid brings the course materials. Access to your actual work area for examples is helpful but not required.

### What is the minimum group size?

There's no strict minimum. On-site classes are most cost-effective for groups of about five or more. Smaller groups can join a shared session.

### Can several companies share one class?

Yes. General contractors often bring their subcontractors together, and small companies split a session to share costs.

### How far will you travel?

Zahid is based in Naperville and regularly teaches throughout Chicago and the suburbs in Cook, DuPage, Will, Kane, Lake, and McHenry counties. Travel elsewhere in Illinois and other states is available by arrangement.

### Can you train on weekends or night shift?

Often, yes. Include your preferred days and times in the booking form.
