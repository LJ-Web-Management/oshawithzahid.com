## Why learn OSHA safety in Gujarati?

Safety training only works if it's understood. Workers who learn in their first language catch details that get lost in translation: what a guardrail height really means, why a ladder angle matters, what a chemical label is warning about. They also ask more questions, which is where real learning happens.

OSHA itself requires that training be presented in a language and vocabulary workers understand. A Gujarati-language class is one of the clearest ways to meet that expectation for Gujarati-speaking crews.

### Is an OSHA card from a Gujarati-language class official?

Yes. OSHA allows authorized trainers to teach Outreach courses in languages other than English when the trainer is fluent. The card is the same U.S. Department of Labor wallet card, and it is printed in English.

### Can my crew mix Gujarati and English speakers in one class?

Yes. Zahid can teach in English and explain key points in Gujarati (or Urdu) so everyone in a mixed group follows along.
