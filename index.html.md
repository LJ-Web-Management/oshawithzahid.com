### Should my workers take OSHA 10 or OSHA 30?

OSHA 10 is designed for entry-level workers and covers core hazard recognition. OSHA 30 is designed for supervisors, foremen, and anyone with safety responsibility. Many employers put the whole crew through OSHA 10 and their leads through OSHA 30. See our [OSHA 10 vs OSHA 30 guide](https://oshawithzahid.com/guides/osha-10-vs-osha-30.html) for details.
