Frequently asked questions

Who is Zahid Presswala?

Zahid Presswala is an OSHA-authorized Outreach Trainer for Construction and General Industry, based in Naperville, Illinois, with more than 25 years of hands-on field experience. He is listed in OSHA's official Outreach Trainer directory and teaches in English, Gujarati, and Urdu.

Do your courses come with an official OSHA card?

Yes. Every OSHA 10-Hour and 30-Hour course is taught under the OSHA Outreach Training Program and ends with an official U.S. Department of Labor (DOL) wallet card for each student who completes it.

Where do you offer OSHA training?

Training is based in Naperville, IL and delivered on-site at your job site, shop, or office anywhere in Chicagoland, including Chicago, Aurora, Joliet, and the western suburbs. On-site travel outside Illinois is available by arrangement.

Should my workers take OSHA 10 or OSHA 30?

OSHA 10 is designed for entry-level workers and covers core hazard recognition. OSHA 30 is designed for supervisors, foremen, and anyone with safety responsibility. Many employers put the whole crew through OSHA 10 and their leads through OSHA 30. See our OSHA 10 vs OSHA 30 guide for details.

Do you offer group pricing?

Yes. Group pricing is available for crews of any size. Include your headcount in the booking form and you will get a custom quote.

Can training be taught in Gujarati or Urdu?

Yes. Zahid teaches in English, Gujarati, and Urdu, so crews can learn in the language they understand best.
