Why take OSHA training in Urdu?

Many workers can get by in English on the job, but safety training is where "getting by" isn't enough. Learning in Urdu means workers understand why a rule exists, not just what it says, and they're far more likely to speak up when something looks wrong.

OSHA expects employers to present training in a language workers understand. An Urdu-language Outreach course meets that expectation and gives every worker the same official card.

Frequently asked questions

Can I take OSHA 10 in Hindi?

Zahid teaches in Urdu, which shares everyday spoken vocabulary and grammar with Hindi. Most Hindi speakers follow an Urdu-language class easily. Technical terms are explained in plain language, with the English OSHA term alongside.

Is the OSHA card from an Urdu class the same as an English one?

Yes. It is the same official U.S. Department of Labor Outreach wallet card. The card itself is printed in English.

Do you offer Urdu OSHA training outside Chicago?

Yes, by arrangement. Share your location and group size through the booking form.
