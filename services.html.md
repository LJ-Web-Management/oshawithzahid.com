## Questions about OSHA courses

### How long does OSHA 10 take?

OSHA 10 is 10 contact hours. OSHA limits Outreach training to 7.5 hours per day, so an OSHA 10 class runs over at least two days. Many on-site classes are scheduled as two consecutive days.

### How long does OSHA 30 take?

OSHA 30 is 30 contact hours. With the 7.5-hour daily limit, it takes at least four days. On-site classes can be scheduled on consecutive days or spread over several weeks to fit your crew's schedule.

### Do I need OSHA 10 before taking OSHA 30?

No. OSHA 30 is a standalone course. Supervisors and safety leads can go straight into OSHA 30 without taking OSHA 10 first.

### When will students receive their DOL cards?

OSHA requires authorized trainers to issue cards within 90 days of course completion. Students receive a completion record at the end of class and the official wallet card follows.

### What is the difference between Construction and General Industry?

Construction courses follow 29 CFR 1926 and cover building, renovation, demolition, and similar work. General Industry courses follow 29 CFR 1910 and cover warehouses, manufacturing, healthcare, retail, and most other fixed workplaces. See our [Construction vs General Industry guide](https://oshawithzahid.com/guides/construction-vs-general-industry.html).
