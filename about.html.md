## Field-tested safety training, not just a slide deck

I'm Zahid Presswala, an OSHA-authorized Outreach Trainer based in Naperville, Illinois. Before I ever stepped into a classroom, I spent 25+ years working construction and industrial job sites, which means the hazards I teach about aren't theoretical to me. I've seen what happens when safety procedures are skipped, and I've seen how the right training prevents it.

Today I teach OSHA 10 and 30-Hour Outreach courses for both Construction and General Industry. Every course meets OSHA Outreach Training Program requirements while staying grounded in how crews actually work day to day. Sessions run on-site at your location, at a shared venue, or as custom workshops built around your trade.

- OSHA Outreach authorized trainer: [verified listing on OSHA.gov](https://www.osha.gov/dte/outreach/outreach_trainers?page=0&last_name=Presswala)
- Authorized for Construction and General Industry Outreach courses
- 25+ years of hands-on construction and field safety experience
- Instruction in English, Gujarati, and Urdu
- Based in Naperville, IL, serving Chicagoland and available for on-site travel
