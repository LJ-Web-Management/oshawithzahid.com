OSHA 10-Hour Construction topics

OSHA 10 Construction follows the construction standards in 29 CFR 1926. The course outline includes:

SectionTopics
RequiredIntroduction to OSHA (2 hours): worker rights, employer responsibilities, filing a complaint, inspections
RequiredOSHA Focus Four hazards (4 hours): falls, electrocution, struck-by, caught-in or between
RequiredPersonal protective and lifesaving equipment
RequiredHealth hazards in construction: noise, silica, lead, chemicals, heat
ElectivesChosen by the trainer, such as cranes and rigging, excavations, materials handling, scaffolds, stairways and ladders, tools, or fire protection
OptionalAny construction topic that fits the class, or more time on required topics

OSHA 10-Hour General Industry topics

OSHA 10 General Industry follows 29 CFR 1910. The course outline includes:

SectionTopics
RequiredIntroduction to OSHA (2 hours)
RequiredWalking and working surfaces, including fall protection
RequiredExit routes, emergency action plans, fire prevention plans, and fire protection
RequiredElectrical safety
RequiredPersonal protective equipment
RequiredHazard communication
ElectivesSuch as hazardous materials, materials handling, machine guarding, lockout/tagout, powered industrial trucks, ergonomics, bloodborne pathogens, or safety and health programs

How OSHA 30 compares

OSHA 30 covers everything above in more depth and adds a much longer list of topics, along with safety management for supervisors. Read OSHA 10 vs OSHA 30 to choose.

What OSHA 10 doesn't cover

OSHA 10 is an awareness course. It does not qualify anyone to operate a forklift, erect a scaffold, enter a confined space, or serve as a "competent person." Employers must still provide the task- and site-specific training OSHA standards require.
