What Downers Grove employers should know

Healthcare and office facilities are General Industry workplaces where electrical safety, hazard communication, walking-working surfaces, and bloodborne pathogens matter most. Contractors renovating those buildings work under Construction rules. OSHA 10 is a common requirement for contractors working on hospital campuses.

Plan ahead: a legitimate OSHA 10 takes at least two days and OSHA 30 at least four, because OSHA limits Outreach training to 7.5 hours per day. Any "one-day OSHA 10" offer is a red flag. See how to spot a fake card.
