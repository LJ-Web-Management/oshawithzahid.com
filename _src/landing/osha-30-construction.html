What is OSHA 30-Hour Construction?

OSHA 30-Hour Construction is the supervisor-level course in OSHA's Outreach Training Program. Where OSHA 10 teaches a worker to protect themselves, OSHA 30 teaches a leader to protect a crew: spotting hazards before work starts, planning around them, and building the habits that keep a site out of trouble with OSHA.

What the course covers

OSHA 30 Construction includes all the core topics from OSHA 10, in more depth, plus a wider range of construction hazards and safety management topics:

  • Introduction to OSHA, worker rights, employer duties, and how inspections and citations work
  • Managing safety and health: programs, inspections, accident investigation, and working with subcontractors
  • The Focus Four in depth: falls, electrocution, struck-by, and caught-in or between
  • Personal protective and lifesaving equipment and health hazards such as silica, noise, lead, and heat
  • Stairways and ladders, scaffolds, excavations and trenching
  • Cranes, derricks, hoists, and rigging
  • Concrete and masonry, steel erection, materials handling, and tools
  • Electives chosen for your work, such as confined spaces, fire protection, motor vehicles, or welding and cutting

Who should take OSHA 30 Construction?

  • Foremen, lead hands, and crew chiefs
  • Superintendents and assistant superintendents
  • Project managers and owners of small contracting companies
  • Safety coordinators and anyone designated to run a site safety program
  • Experienced workers stepping up to a lead role

A common pattern for Chicago-area contractors: the whole crew takes OSHA 10, and every lead takes OSHA 30.

Scheduling OSHA 30 around your projects

The hardest part of OSHA 30 is usually pulling supervisors off the job for four days. On-site training solves that with flexible scheduling:

  • Four or five consecutive days during a slow week
  • One day a week for four to five weeks
  • Shared sessions with supervisors from partner companies to split cost

Whichever schedule you choose, OSHA requires the course to be completed within six months, and Zahid will plan the dates with you up front.
