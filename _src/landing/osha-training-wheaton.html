What Wheaton employers should know

Wheaton has a high share of public-sector employers. Those workers are covered by Illinois OSHA, the state plan run by the Illinois Department of Labor, rather than federal OSHA. Illinois OSHA's standards must be at least as effective as federal OSHA's, so the same OSHA 10 and 30 courses apply. Public works crews doing construction-type work typically take Construction; facilities and grounds staff take General Industry.

Plan ahead: a legitimate OSHA 10 takes at least two days and OSHA 30 at least four, because OSHA limits Outreach training to 7.5 hours per day. Any "one-day OSHA 10" offer is a red flag. See how to spot a fake card.
