What Elgin employers should know

In manufacturing, the most frequently cited OSHA standards include lockout/tagout, machine guarding, hazard communication, and powered industrial trucks. OSHA 30 General Industry gives Elgin supervisors the knowledge to find and correct those hazards before an inspector does.

Plan ahead: a legitimate OSHA 10 takes at least two days and OSHA 30 at least four, because OSHA limits Outreach training to 7.5 hours per day. Any "one-day OSHA 10" offer is a red flag. See how to spot a fake card.
