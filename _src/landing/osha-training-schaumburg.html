What Schaumburg employers should know

Hotels, retail, and office buildings are General Industry workplaces. Their biggest OSHA risks are often less obvious than a construction site's: ladders and slips, electrical work by maintenance staff, chemicals used in housekeeping and pools, and lockout/tagout on building equipment. Contractors working inside those buildings fall under Construction rules, so many Schaumburg properties need both courses.

Plan ahead: a legitimate OSHA 10 takes at least two days and OSHA 30 at least four, because OSHA limits Outreach training to 7.5 hours per day. Any "one-day OSHA 10" offer is a red flag. See how to spot a fake card.
