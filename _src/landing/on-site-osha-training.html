How on-site OSHA training works

  1. Tell us what you need. Use the booking form to share your industry, headcount, location, language, and dates.
  2. Get a quote and a plan. Zahid confirms the right course, picks electives that match your hazards, and sets a schedule that fits your shifts.
  3. Train on-site. Your team completes the course where they work, with examples drawn from your equipment and conditions.
  4. Get carded. Each graduate gets proof of completion, and official DOL wallet cards follow (OSHA requires them within 90 days).
