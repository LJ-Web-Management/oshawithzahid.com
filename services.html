OSHA training programs for Construction & General Industry

Four authorized Outreach courses, each delivered on-site or in group sessions and finished with an official DOL wallet card.

S1.1Course Spec Sheets
Course code C‑1010 Hours
Construction10 Hours

OSHA 10-Hour Construction Training

An entry-level course covering the most common construction hazards and worker rights, ideal for new hires and anyone required to carry a 10-hour card.

Covers
  • Fall protection, electrical, and struck-by hazards
  • PPE, health hazards & worker rights
  • Official DOL 10-hour wallet card
Course code C‑3030 Hours
Construction30 Hours

OSHA 30-Hour Construction Training

An in-depth course for supervisors, foremen, and safety personnel who oversee job-site hazard prevention and crew safety programs.

Covers
  • Extended fall protection & excavation standards
  • Managing subcontractor & crew safety
  • Official DOL 30-hour wallet card
Course code GI‑1010 Hours
General Industry10 Hours

OSHA 10-Hour General Industry Training

Foundational safety training for warehouse, manufacturing, and facility employees covering the most frequently cited general industry hazards.

Covers
  • Walking-working surfaces & emergency action plans
  • Machine guarding, electrical & hazard communication
  • Official DOL 10-hour wallet card
Course code GI‑3030 Hours
General Industry30 Hours

OSHA 30-Hour General Industry Training

A comprehensive safety management course for supervisors and safety coordinators responsible for facility-wide compliance programs.

Covers
  • Permit-required confined spaces & lockout/tagout
  • Ergonomics, recordkeeping & program management
  • Official DOL 30-hour wallet card
